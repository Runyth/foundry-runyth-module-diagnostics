### Resolution mapping

- Duplicated asset roots: fixed by canonical package-path normalization before route generation and cache-key creation.
- Compatibility exception: fixed by defensive reads from the modern `compatibility` object without deprecated pre-V10 core-version fallbacks.
- Misleading console source locations: fixed by removing permanent background console wrappers. Focused capture wraps only explicitly requested levels and stores a caller stack with diagnostics-internal frames removed.
- Scan-completion noise: removed. Completion is represented by persistent scan-card results and the reserved progress region.
- Browser log truncation: the diagnostics-generated 994-entry false 404 flood is eliminated; genuine failed requests are intentionally not hidden.

The six non-duplicated 404 entries refer to Greensock assets under `global-progress-clocks` and `battle-transitions`. They are not rewritten or suppressed because the supplied log does not establish that they are defects in Runyth Module Diagnostics.
